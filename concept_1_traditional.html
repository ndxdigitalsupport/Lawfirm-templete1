About Us

Founded on the ethos of integrity, honesty, compassion and excellence — core values which guide our practice and relationships. We are a premier boutique legal office dedicated to providing clients with sound, practical and cost-effective legal advice and representation on commercial corporate, family, real estate and dispute resolution (commercial & criminal litigation and commercial arbitration).

Our team is active, precise and result-oriented. Our craft lies in legal representation and sound legal advice honed over the trust of our clients. We adopt nimble, agile, creative and client-centric approaches to tailor bespoke solutions for our clients’ specific needs.

Areas of Expertise

Corporate / M&A

Real Estate

Protecting commercial developers and investors through complex transactions and dispute resolution.

Litigation

Aggressive representation in both commercial and criminal litigation, achieving optimal client outcomes.

Client Services

Legal Consultation
